How to use

  1. Place the mattress on a suitable base. It is a mattress, not a bed frame.
  2. Connect the pump and follow the instructions supplied with the product.
  3. Check that the mattress is inflated as described in those instructions.
  4. Clean the cover according to the instructions supplied with the product.

Follow the cleaning instructions supplied with the product. Do not use a method that is not listed there.
