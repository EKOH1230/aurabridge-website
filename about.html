AuraBridge is the bridge between Asian beauty and care devices and professional buyers in Europe.

We are a B2B wholesale brand for salons, distributors and care buyers in France and the wider European market.

Our work

What AuraBridge does

We focus on wholesale supply of professional devices, not on consumer retail.

We supply scalp-care tools and a care mattress. Buyers can review the models, request a quote, and ask for a sample.

We describe products with confirmed facts only. Beauty devices and the care mattress are kept in separate groups, because they are used in different settings.

How we work

A straightforward way to request supply.

These are the steps we follow when a professional buyer gets in touch.

01

Quotes by email

There is no account to create. The form opens your email app, and we reply by email.

02

Confirmed facts only

We describe each model with details we can stand behind. We do not claim medical effects or certifications that have not been confirmed.

03

Two product groups

Scalp-care tools and the care mattress are presented separately, because they serve different buyers.
